About Bible Mood Matcher

Bible Mood Matcher is a spiritual companion that connects your emotional state with the comforting, encouraging, and transformative words of Scripture.

We believe God's Word speaks to every heart in every season — joy, anger, sadness, gratitude, or anxiety. Select a mood, swipe through handpicked verses with deep reflections, save favourites, and journal personal notes.

Reading plans guide you through multi-day devotional journeys, and Prayer Focus Mode lets you meditate on a single verse in stillness.

All scriptures from the New International Version (NIV). © 2026 Bible Mood Matcher
